Privacy policy

Safe Duplicate Finder

The app collects nothing and has no network connection of any kind.

Updated 20 August 2026. Applies to version 1.0 and later.

No account, no sign in, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising. The app does not ask Windows for internet access, so it has no way to send anything anywhere.

What it reads

Only the folders you choose, and only during a scan. It reads file contents to prove two files are identical, because that is the point of the product. Nothing read is written down, kept or sent.

What it stores

Two local settings, neither about your files: the appearance you chose, and whether the welcome screen has been shown. Both are removed when you uninstall. There is no log and no scan history.

What it deletes

Only the copies you tick. One copy of every set always stays, and everything removed goes to the Recycle Bin.

The optional purchase

Duplicate Folders is handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app asks Windows one question, whether this account owns it, and gets yes or no. It never sees your name, email or payment details. Microsoft's part is covered by the Microsoft privacy statement.

What Microsoft reports

As with every Store app, Microsoft gives the developer install counts, ratings and anonymous crash data. That comes from the Store, not from the app, and it contains nothing about your files.

Children

A general purpose utility. It collects no personal information from anybody, of any age.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, the page is updated before that version ships, and the date above changes with it.
