Privacy policy

Safe Duplicate Finder

The app collects nothing and makes no network connections.

Updated 6 November 2026. Applies to version 1.0 and later.

No account, no sign in, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising. The app's code makes no network connections and sends nothing anywhere.

What it reads

Only the folders you choose: during a scan, before a removal, and for Backup Cleanup, the re-check on launch. It reads file contents to prove two files are identical, because that is the point of the product. File contents are never written down or sent. Backup Cleanup keeps paths and hashes in a local record of each job, so the job can be undone.

What it stores

A few local settings, none about your files: the appearance, whether the welcome screen was shown, and your scan choices. They are removed when you uninstall. If an item goes by the short folder route, a local record of its path (short-route.jsonl) stays until the bin no longer holds it. If you use Backup Cleanup, the app also keeps, on your own computer, a record of each job so it can be undone, a CSV and HTML report of it, and the date it last checked the folder you keep, so it can check again on the first launch 30 days or more later. These stay on your computer, are never sent anywhere, and you can delete them yourself. Apart from these, the app keeps no scan history.

What it deletes

Only the copies and folders you choose. This app never removes the last copy of a set, and everything removed goes to the Recycle Bin. Backup Cleanup sends the old folder to the Recycle Bin only when nothing unique is left in it. Removal needs a drive with a Recycle Bin. Most USB sticks, SD cards and network drives have none, so they are compared but not cleaned.

The optional purchase

Backup Cleanup (USD 14.99 once) and Backup Cleanup Monthly (USD 4.99 a month) are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app asks Windows whether this account owns either one, and what each costs in your money, for the prices on its buttons. It never sees your name, email or payment details. Microsoft's part is covered by the Microsoft privacy statement.

What Microsoft reports

As with every Store app, Microsoft gives the developer install counts, ratings and anonymous crash data. That comes from the Store, not from the app, and it contains nothing about your files.

Children

A general purpose utility. It collects no personal information from anybody, of any age.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, the page is updated before that version ships, and the date above changes with it.
